⚡ How it works

Add songs — or drop them anywhere on the page — and the first one starts itself. Kicks launch fireworks, the singer's voice blooms a pink heart. Collapse the panel with the – for movie mode 🎬 — and keep an eye on the moon: a shadow cat lives up there. 🐈‍⬛

🧭 Journey travels between the Curtains, the Tree and Synapse as the song moves.

🪐 Music Orbit is the real solar system, with every planet where it is today, under the lyrics. Each planet glows with its own part of the song, deep sounds near the Sun and airy ones at the edge. The Sun breathes with the bass, the planets swell a little on the kick, a calm slow song turns it romantic (threads of light between the planets), and a big moment sends a ring out from the Sun and the whole system on a trip, then back: flying through the galaxy, or out to the true distances. DIM in the panel sets how dark it sits behind the words.

🛰️ Above the Storms is the night side of the Earth from a space station: looking the way it flies: the Milky Way and the stars rising slowly, the airglow along the curve, cloud and golden city lights coming up over the horizon and sliding away underneath, the station across the top. The singer's voice lifts the airglow, the top end of the song sets the stars twinkling, and the tempo sets the pace. The kick lights the clouds from inside, small soft flashes of lightning: more of them as the song builds, a chain of clouds one after another at a big moment (with a gold glint along the station's wings, and now and then a rare violet sprite over a storm), and only a rare soft glow in a slow song. Aboard, the arm moves between poses now and then and a few small lights come and go. With Reduce Motion there are no flashes, only a slow faint glow in a cloud now and then, and the station holds still.

🎤 Mic turns the visuals onto the room: music from another speaker, a band, or your own voice. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere, and nothing is played back.

♫ Find lyrics looks the playing song up in LRCLIB, a free lyrics library online, and remembers what it finds. A .lrc file works too: the .lrc button, or drop it on the page.
