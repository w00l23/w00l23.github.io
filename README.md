# w00l23.github.io

The home page of Mina Systems. One plain HTML file, no frameworks.

The hero is the real sky over Houston at this minute, drawn on a 2D canvas:
the Yale Bright Star Catalogue to magnitude 5.6 (packed into the page, 26 KB),
the planets from JPL's Keplerian elements and the Moon from Meeus, the same
sources as the solar-system demo. Drag to look around, tap a star for its name,
"Use my sky" asks for your location. From late November to Twelfth Night the
skyline wears a string of lights (`?lights=1` shows them any day; `?t=` previews
another hour, e.g. `?t=2026-12-24T21:00:00-06:00`).

**Live:** https://w00l23.github.io/

Links out to the live demos ([Solar System](https://w00l23.github.io/solar-system/),
[Wormhole](https://w00l23.github.io/wormhole/), [Deep Field](https://w00l23.github.io/deep-field/))
and to [Iluminara](https://www.iluminara-messenger.com).

`assets/` holds the card previews and the Iluminara screenshots.
